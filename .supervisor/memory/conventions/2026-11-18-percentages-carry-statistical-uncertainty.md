Any percentage or rate in waveform-analysis studies, PDFs, TSVs and the write-up is quoted with its statistical uncertainty, such as a Wilson 68 % interval or binomial error.
Why: the user's standing instruction ("When calculating the percentage, always include statistical uncertainty").
Rejected: quoting bare percentages.
